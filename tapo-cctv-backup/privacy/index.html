Tapo CCTV Backup - Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

Who uses the app

The app is used only by its owner, for their own Google account.

Google permissions

The app requests only the Google Drive "drive.file" permission, so it can only see and manage files it created itself.

Data handling

The app uploads video files recorded by the owner's cameras to the owner's own Google Drive. No data is collected, sold, shared with third parties, or sent anywhere else.

Retention

Uploaded files are deleted automatically after 7 days.

Contact

For privacy questions, email prem314.game.dev@gmail.com.
